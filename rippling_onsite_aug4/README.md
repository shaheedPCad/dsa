## Interview Loop

Before coding, say:

```text
Let me restate the problem.
What are the inputs and outputs?
Are there edge cases like empty input, duplicates, disconnected graphs, or cycles?
What is the brute force approach?
What is the optimized invariant?
I'll code that, then trace examples and edge cases.
```

For graph problems, always ask:

```text
What are the nodes?
What are the edges?
Directed or undirected?
Weighted or unweighted?
Traversal, shortest path, ordering, or grouping?
Can there be cycles?
Can it be disconnected?
```
